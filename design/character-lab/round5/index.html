ROUND 5 · FIVE THOUGHTFUL VERSIONS

Five ways to think.
Look, weigh, remember, focus, wait.

Five versions of Nibbi, each built around one faculty of a thoughtful companion. Talk to them.

Regard looks before it moves. Keel weighs both sides and rights itself. Tide keeps what just happened as a stain that soaks away. Contour draws its own depth of focus. Pebble turns toward you and stays. Same nine moments, same three sizes, same paper; a different idea of a pause under each one.

HOW TO LOOK
  1. Type in the composer. Does it listen while you type, take a beat, then answer?
  2. Leave Think running. Considering, or fidgeting?
  3. Error after Success. Owned, or hidden?
  4. Squint at the chat strip. Can you tell it is paying attention beside text?
  5. Reduced motion on. Is a considered still left?
