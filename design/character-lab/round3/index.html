ROUND 3 · MORE LIKE THE BEAD

Six lit, raymarched premises. The bead you liked, plus five relatives.

Same technique — a real 3D object on the paper, lit and shadowed — new ideas: ink swirling inside a glass marble, an inkwell with a lid, a drop clinging to a pen nib, a velvet-matte soot ball, and a bead that splits into a colony. Press, drag and poke each one.
