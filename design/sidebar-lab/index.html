THE SIDEBAR LAB

Chat is at the bottom
of the bar. Where does it belong?

Four bars, the same eight projects, the same nine moments — side by side.

The first is the bar you have today. The other three each answer one question: what is the project control, and where do conversations sit relative to Builds, Issues and Plans?

Interactive prototypes. The app is not changed, and nothing here talks to the daemon.

HOW TO LOOK
  1. Start at the phone strip. 390px with twelve projects is where a layout fails.
  2. Count clicks to a second conversation, then to another project's.
  3. Press 8. Collapsed, what does the bar still tell you?
  4. Press 6. Busy: New thread is gone. Does the layout still make sense?
  5. Turn the improvements on one at a time. They are separable from the layout.
